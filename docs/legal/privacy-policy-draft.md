## 1. What this service is

[Product name] is an AI interview-practice service for adults (18+) preparing for their own job interviews. It is **not** used to make hiring decisions about anyone.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account details (email, name, password hash) | Run your account | Consent / contract |
| Resume text you upload, **with contact details removed** before any AI processing | Tailor interview questions | Consent (data processing) |
| Your answers (transcripts) and evaluations | Give you feedback and history | Consent (data processing) |
| Answer audio | Transcribe, analyse pace and pauses, and run the checks below. **Not stored** unless you turn on "Keep recordings" | Consent (data processing) |
| Camera signals computed **on your device**: number of faces, mouth movement, whether you face the screen, whether a phone is visible | Practice-integrity notices and on-screen feedback. **Video is not sent to us** | Consent (data processing) |
| Optional face template (from short face images captured during set-up and periodic checks) | Check the same person stays in the session | Separate consent (face check) |
| Optional voice template (from three phrases you read) | Check the same person is answering | Separate consent (voice check) |
| Usage and cost records, technical logs (with personal data masked) | Operate, secure and bill the service | Legitimate use / contract |
| Payment status from Stripe or Razorpay (we never see your card details) | Subscriptions | Contract |

We do **not**:
- infer emotions, personality or protected characteristics;
- sell personal data;
- use your data to train models unless you separately opt in.

## 3. Biometric data

Face and voice templates are numeric representations, encrypted with keys held in a cloud key-management service. They are not photos or recordings and cannot be turned back into them. They are deleted after [30] days, when you withdraw the consent, or when you delete your account, whichever comes first.

## 6. Your rights

From Privacy settings you can download everything we hold, delete your face and voice templates, change any consent, and delete your account. You can also ask us to correct your data, nominate someone to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance with our grievance officer. If unresolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, hashed passwords, access controls, audit logging. If a personal data breach occurs we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required by law.
