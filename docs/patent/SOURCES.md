## Abstract (verbatim element list, used as the provisional claim basis)

> ...an AI-powered virtual interview coaching system comprising
> 1. a **user interface module** for receiving candidate information and job roles,
> 2. a **facial recognition module** for capturing facial samples and verifying candidate identity,
> 3. a **voice authentication module** for recording voice references and performing real-time voice matching,
> 4. a **lip-sync verification module** for analyzing mouth movements and detecting speech authenticity mismatches,
> 5. an **eye-tracking module** for monitoring gaze direction and engagement levels,
> 6. a **natural language processing module** for parsing resumes, generating personalized interview questions, and evaluating responses using transformer-based models,
> 7. a **security monitoring module** for detecting unauthorized devices and preventing assistance,
> 8. a **technical assessment module** for presenting coding challenges and providing feedback, and
> 9. a **performance evaluation module** for analyzing transcripts and generating comprehensive reports.

Field of invention [0003] adds: multimodal authentication "including facial recognition, voice matching, lip-sync verification, and real-time behavioral monitoring", "dynamic question generation and technical assessment".
